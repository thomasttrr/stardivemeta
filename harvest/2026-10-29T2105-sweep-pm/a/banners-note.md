## Pool evidence (why the verdict is PARTIAL)

- **Permanent 5★ (the "standard six"):** Jiwon, Flare, Sangun, Ophelia,
  Gabi, Penny — the beginner-banner / 5★-selector pool, per
  mongilstardive.wiki's selector and reroll guides ("the beginner banner
  only contains the six standard 5-star characters (Jiwon, Flare, Sangun,
  Ophelia, Gabi, and Penny)"). Two pages, **one site** — counts as one
  source family. GameWith JP's criteria caveat ("permanent 5★ up to A2")
  confirms a permanent 5★ class exists but does not name its members.
- **Limited:** Esther ("limited-time character … only from her Limited
  Recruitment banner" — mongilstardive.wiki; official showcase calls her
  "the first event summon character"); Narae (Game8 explicitly labels hers
  a "Limited Banner"); Summer pair (seasonal event units; official Q&A
  says reruns are *planned*, i.e. not in the permanent pool); Mina, Nagi,
  Mabel, Brisshell, Vivian, Isabella, Chloe (each debuted on a time-limited
  event banner using Compass of Destiny per Prydwen's banner rules; Mina/
  Narae/Esther/Nagi have had announced *reruns*, which only makes sense
  for limited units). No source explicitly says "limited" for Mabel,
  Brisshell, Vivian, Isabella, Chloe individually.
- **4★ pool:** all twelve 4★ appear as rate-ups / standard-pool members;
  no source marks any 4★ limited. Francis is a free pre-registration
  reward; Reina is tutorial-guaranteed; Ophelia (5★) was given free in
  v1.1 despite being standard-pool.
- **Bottom line for CP2:** pool is *probably* {limited: Esther + all 10
  post-launch 5★} / {permanent: standard six 5★ + all 4★}, but it is not
  two-source verified per character. Per T2.8, if it can't be confirmed
  for all 29 the fields stay omitted → **reroll section stays off (D-3)**.
  Recommended follow-up: Kaiden character pages may carry pool data in
  their full text (the two pages read in full — Mina, Brisshell — did
  not), or the in-game banner history; neither was exhaustively read here.
