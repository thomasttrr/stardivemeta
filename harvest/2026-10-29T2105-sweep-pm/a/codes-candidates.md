## Confirmed-active list (proposed)

1. **HAPPYHALF1014** — Compass of Destiny ×3, Compass of Memory ×3, Compass of
   Promise ×3, Special Gold Upgrade Stone ×1. Officially announced in the
   Oct 14, 2026 Developer Lounge Talk (official forum write-up, via Kaiden
   recap). **Expiry text: "Valid until Nov. 10, 2026, 23:30 (UTC)"** (COSM
   agrees: released Oct 14, expires Nov 10, 2026). Strongest code in the set —
   official witness + COSM active (checked Oct 27).
2. **REGINULAUPDATE** — 10 Premium Salmon Puree, 1 Purple Upgrade Stone,
   3 Compass of Promise (SuperCheats says 5 Compass — wording conflict noted,
   majority text used). Active: PG-group, Dexerto, SuperCheats, GamsGo, COSM,
   Pocket Tactics. No expiry stated anywhere.
3. **LETSPLAYEP6** — same reward text as REGINULAUPDATE. Active: PG-group,
   Dexerto, SuperCheats, GamsGo, COSM. No expiry stated.
4. **LOVEMONGIL** — 1 Overflowing Love (artifact). Active: PG-group, Dexerto,
   GW-EN, SuperCheats, GamsGo, COSM, GamesRadar, Pocket Tactics. One stale
   dissent: GamingOnPhone listed it expired on Aug 3, 2026 — every fresher
   source (Sep 20–Oct 27) says active, so the dissent is recorded but does
   not block. No expiry stated.
5. **MEMOLMONGIL** — Green Cappy Papa, 25 Keys, 10,000 Gold (PG/GamsGo/COSM/
   GamesRadar wording). Active: PG-group, Dexerto, SuperCheats, GamesRadar,
   Pocket Tactics, GamingOnPhone; GamsGo flags it "reported active".
   ⚠ SuperCheats prints a **different reward** for this code ("100 Keys,
   3 Conquest Entry Tickets, and 3 Compass of Promise" — that is
   WINDSOFFATE/NAGIUPDATE's reward per Kaiden): SuperCheats' reward text is
   unreliable; its active/expired *status* is still counted. Dexerto omits
   the Green Cappy Papa from the reward. No expiry stated.
6. **GSBGMONGIL** — Uncle Cappy ×1, 10,000 Gold (GamsGo adds the Gold;
   GW-EN/GamesRadar list the Monsterling only). Active: GamsGo, COSM
   (checked Oct 27), GW-EN (confirmed Apr 16 — old), GamesRadar (stale).
   No expired claims. Freshness caveat: only COSM/GamsGo are recent.
7. **JOINMONGIL** — Leafymander ×1. Active: GamsGo, COSM (Oct 27), GW-EN
   (Apr 16 — old), GamesRadar (stale). No expired claims. Same freshness
   caveat as GSBGMONGIL.
