## Future candidate: See-through layer decomposition (checked 2026-10-30, Tom's tip)
- Repo: github.com/shitagaki-lab/see-through — SIGGRAPH 2026 paper, Apache-2.0, ~5.2k stars. Single anime illustration -> up to 23 fully-inpainted semantic layers (hair/face/eyes/clothing/accessories) with depth order, exported as layered PSD. Tech: LayerDiff 3D (SDXL) + Marigold anime depth.
- Runs locally: 12–16 GB VRAM at 1280px (bf16); ~10 GB with offload, ~8 GB NF4-quantized. Tom's RTX 5070 Ti (16 GB) can run it full quality. Free HuggingFace demo: 1–2 PSDs/day at 1280.
- Authors: NOT image-to-Live2D by itself (no rigging). Ecosystem tools close the gap: StretchyStudio (free browser auto-rig puppet animation from the PSD), Anime2.5DRig (blink/lip-sync/hair physics), PNGAL (Windows, exports WebM/MP4/GIF).
- Best site use if ever approved: 2.5D parallax on character pages (layers shift at different depths) — the premium gacha-site feel.
- POLICY FLAG: layers are AI-inpainted — hidden areas (body under clothing, hair behind shoulders) are invented pixels. That is transformation OF the art, the category the fan-content rules target (presentation AROUND art is the safe side). Using it on official art is Tom's explicit call, not assumed. Parked as a future candidate 2026-10-30.
